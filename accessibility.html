Our commitment

GrubMarket is committed to making its websites, marketplace, and software accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We want every customer to be able to source, sell, and order food with ease.

Standards we follow

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA and to align with applicable requirements such as the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

What we do

  • Support keyboard navigation and visible focus across interactive elements. Menus open with Enter, Space or Arrow Down, close with Escape, and expose their state through aria-expanded.
  • Use semantic markup and ARIA labels so assistive technologies can interpret content.
  • Maintain color contrast at WCAG AA (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for focus indicators) and scalable, legible typography.
  • Provide text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Respect the operating system's reduced-motion setting.

Known limitations

We test this site continuously and publish what we have not yet resolved:

  • Mobile touch-target sizes have not been audited against the 44 × 44 px minimum.
  • The site has not been tested end to end with a screen reader; our checks so far have been automated plus manual keyboard testing.
  • The contact form currently falls back to email if submission fails.

Ongoing effort

Accessibility is an ongoing process. We test regularly, train our teams, and work to remediate issues as we identify them.

Feedback

If you encounter a barrier or need content in an alternative format, please tell us through our contact page and we'll work to help.
