Why it costs less than the grocery store

Traditional grocery pricing carries the cost of a distributor, a wholesaler, and a retail markup before food ever reaches a shelf. GrubMarket's delivery hubs source directly from the same grower and distributor network that supplies the B2B marketplace, so a box of produce skips several of those markups on its way to your door — which is how prices land up to 50% below grocery store prices for comparable quality.

It's also the same infrastructure GrubMarket built for wholesale: the trucks, the cold storage, and the grower relationships behind Good Eggs, SPUD, FreshGOGO, and DO Organics are the same ones behind pallets shipped to restaurants and grocers — home delivery is simply a second use of a supply chain GrubMarket already runs at scale.
