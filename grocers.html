Skip the layers between you and the grower

A typical grocer or restaurant buys through a chain of intermediaries — grower, packer, regional wholesaler, broker — each adding a markup before the product reaches a receiving dock. GrubMarket's network compresses that chain: sourcing runs through the same grower and distributor relationships that supply the wholesale marketplace, including established regional players like Coast Citrus Distributors and Schoenmann Produce, so pricing reflects fewer hands touching the product.

Ordering itself runs through the Orders IO app or the web catalog, both synced to real-time inventory on WholesaleWare — so what you see available is what actually ships, not a static price sheet that's already out of date.
